Ember Court

A lane-battle card duel of fire, tide, gale and stone.

Play creatures into three lanes. Spend mana, outwit the Warden, and bring their life to zero. Ember beats Gale, Gale beats Stone, Stone beats Tide, Tide beats Ember — a winning element strikes +1 harder.

Click a card, then a lane. Keys 1–6 pick a card, Enter ends the turn.
